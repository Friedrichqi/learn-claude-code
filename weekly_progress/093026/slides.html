3Result · 1 · Many users, one KV pool · the experiment

N simulated harness users share one H200 running Qwen3-32B: 4-turn sessions, 30 s think pauses, one 258k-token KV pool

The experiment

  • A user = one 4-turn harness session on a real codebase (2 explain, 2 modify turns), with ~30 s think pauses between turns
  • N users, closed loop, on one vLLM server: Qwen3-32B, 40k-token window, one H200 with a 258k-token KV pool (about 8–10 users' contexts)
  • Instrumented scheduler: logs every step, eviction and prefix hit; a fitted step model (R² = 1.000) splits each call's slowdown by cause
  • 502 counted sessions (947 traces with fillers and pilots), 97,498 model calls, 91 GPU-hours; plus 14 trace replays

  • From 12 users, about 10 running requests × 25k tokens fill the 258k pool, so a finished request's blocks are reused almost at once
  • A paused user loses its prefix: at 16–32 users every think pause of 15 s or more loses all of it, and 84–85% of prompt tokens are re-prefilled
  • Thrashing, not a tail effect: at 16–32 users the cache hit rate falls from 86–87% to 1–2%

9Result · 2 · What tool calls cost · the question

A paper puts tool calls at 30–85% of agent latency where we had measured under 1%; we re-measured every tool

The claim

  • Sutradhara (arXiv 2601.12967): tool calls take 30–85% of the time to the final answer (the p50 to p99 of one workload)
  • Its workload: synthetic enterprise requests whose tools are remote services, ~6 s per call
  • Our earlier profiling put tools under 1% of wall time: far below the paper's range

What we ran · C3–C4 queued on Qwen3.8-27B and Qwen3-8B

C4a delay · queuedreal sessions, every tool call slowed to 0.29 / 1.09 / ~6 s

16Result · 2 · What tool calls cost · the claim, its reception and the independent evidence

The 30–85% is plausible for slow remote tools on fast models; a Claude Code harness on thinking models spends 2–29%

17Conclusion

The KV pool, not the tools, limits a shared agent server: gate sessions by KV capacity and stop busting the prefix

What to deploy

1
Admit sessions by KV capacity: at most a pool's worth active gives 3.5–3.8× the throughput (slide 8)
2
Take the per-second timestamp out of the system prompt: at 8 users it cost as much throughput as thrashing (935 calls/h, 18 sessions)
3
Tool execution is not a latency lever here: tools take 0.09% of traced time; it would take 2–9 s per call to matter

What is retired

×
Scheduling knobs as fixes for thrashing: priority and TTL pins soften it (1.2–1.4×); smaller prefill chunks do nothing
×
“Tools take 30–85% of agent latency” for this harness: 50–8,000× what we measure

Not established yet

  • One model and one GPU type for the multi-user study; synthetic think times
  • Tool share with slow tools (injected 0.29–6 s sleeps): six GPU cells pending

Sources: research/11_multiuser_kv/README.md §4; research/06_tool_cost/README.md §C.0, §C.5, §D.4.
