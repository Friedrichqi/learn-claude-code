## Calibration against 11's real offload replays (2026-10-06)

The same simulator on the arrival / admission / finish trace of 11's real replays with vLLM's native 128 GiB CPU
offload (`r_n16_off128`, `r_n32_off128`), next to what those replays recorded (admission hit / arrival ideal).

| trace | real tier: served, % of ideal (from DRAM) | simulated 128 GiB, no cleanup | simulated 128 GiB, dead versions invalidated | simulated 256 GiB |

| r_n16_off128 | 98.5 (86.8) | 98.0 | 99.8 | 99.3 |
| r_n32_off128 | 39.6 (31.1) | 61.6 | 88.8 | 99.1 |

*Reading.*
- At N = 16 the simulator reproduces the real tier (98.0 vs 98.5 %).
- At N = 32 it is 22 points too optimistic on the same trace. Something it does not model also limits the real
  tier. Candidates: the connector's store or load granularity, or its timing under load.
- By the simulator's own decomposition of the real 60-point shortfall at N = 32 (100 − 39.6):
  - dead-version garbage ≈ 27 points (~45 %);
  - capacity beyond garbage ≈ 11 points;
  - the unmodelled gap ≈ 22 points (~36 %).
