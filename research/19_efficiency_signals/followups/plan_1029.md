**The user wants two things:**

- **Part B, GPU:** a test of whether MiniMax-M2.7 calls `wait N seconds` more often when many more users compete.

  - Against that hypothesis: at N=32 the server was already saturated. Under `none` the cache hit rate was 1.4 % and

**The traces are complete** for all 27 analysed cells (16 + x16 in topic 18, 10 in topic 19). Each session has:
- its event trace (turn_start / turn_end, stop decisions, tools, waits, holds, think pauses);
- the per-call capture (t_send / t_recv);
- its meta.

Each cell also has window.json, cell_log.jsonl and the scheduler log. That is 1.3 + 1.1 GB, local and on Drive.

**Already reported:** Kaplan-Meier (KM) turn p50 and p95, in topic 18 README:354-375 and topic 19 README:373-384,
plus the call p50 and mean. **Never reported:** the turn mean, p99, per-session times, intervals and a breakdown by
task type.

**What the exploration found:**
- **Hard stop at the window end.** Every cell stopped hard at its window end, so in-flight turns were cut: up to 69 %
  of the turns started in topic 18's crowded cells, 1-8 % in topic 19. Plain statistics over finished turns are
  therefore biased low; KM handles it.
- **Warm-up sessions are killed inside the window.** They were killed mid-turn, up to 1,224 s into the window.
  - The stored KM and turns/h count those killed turns as completed: in `w32_m_stock`, 22 of its 45 turns/h.
  - Topic 19 has at most 4 such turns per cell.
- **Topic 18's call budgets shape its turns.** Turns are capped at 100 calls and sessions at 300.
  - In `w08_none`, 68 + 17 of 201 turns ended at a cap.
  - After a session's budget is spent, its later turns are refused at once and end "completed" with no work done
    (1-14 per cell).
- **Holds and gate waits are inside turn time.** `turn_start` precedes `maybe_hold("start")`
  (`s15_integrated_harness/code.py:3698-3706, 3915-3935`). Think pauses are outside it.

# Part A · Completion time per task (CPU)

## Step 1 · Definitions

- **Task entry = one turn:** one task prompt, a template E1-E3 or M1-M3 on a codebase.
  - **Completion time:** `turn_start` to `turn_end`, trace wall clock.
  - It includes queueing, every model call, tools, model waits, harness holds and gate waits.
- **Primary sample:** counted sessions only (`meta.filler` false), turns started in [ws, we].
  - **Events:** turns ending before `we` for any reason. Each is classified by its stop decision (`code.py:3749,
    3785, 3796`): normal, turn cap, session cap, or too long.
  - **Censored:** turns cut at `we`, or ending with status "error" inside the window. A turn still held at turn start
    when the window closed counts as cut.
  - **Left out and counted in their own column:** turns refused before any call. These have no `mu_call` event, a
    CallBudget or RunDeadline stop, and were not cut.
  - **Bootstrap cluster:** `meta.spec`, so `S000` and `S000-p2` count as the same task.
- **Sensitivity samples (JSON, plus one Markdown column):**
  - **normal only:** capped and too-long turns are censored;
  - **all sessions:** warm-up sessions included, with their killed turns censored at the kill;
  - **old:** exactly `wa.turn_latencies` (wt_analyze.py:138-156), used only to reproduce the stored values.
- **Secondary units:**
  - **Lead calls:** (i) calls received in the window, which reproduce the stored `lat_p50` and `lat_mean`; (ii) calls
    sent in the window, with in-flight calls at the cut censored (taken from the trace's `mu_call`). Each comes with
    the server's queue / prefill / decode split (mu_analyze.py:399-406).
  - **Sessions:** first turn_start to last turn_end, censored at `we`. Expect "not reached" for topic 18 at N ≥ 16.

## Step 2 · Estimators

- **Mean:** the KM mean, i.e. the area under the curve up to the longest follow-up.
  - It is exact when the curve reaches 0. Otherwise print "≥ x", or "x*" when under 1 % of turns remain.
- **Median, p95 and p99:** KM quantiles, using the rule at wt_analyze.py:76-78.
  - When a quantile is not reached, print "> longest follow-up".
  - Print p99 only when n ≥ 100; mark a quantile † when fewer than 5 turns are expected beyond it.
- **Intervals:** spec-cluster bootstrap, 2,000 draws, seeded per topic and cell.
  - It uses integer cluster weights fed to a vectorized weighted KM.
  - Draws where a quantile is not reached count as +∞, and percentiles use `np.quantile(method="inverted_cdf")`.
- **Ratios against `none`** at the same N:
  - shown only when both statistics are finite in at least 97.5 % of draws;
  - mean ratios use the mean up to the shorter of the two cells' longest follow-ups.
  - Topic 18's `w16_none` against `w16_none_aa` gives the run-to-run (A/A) floor that the bootstrap cannot show.
- **Cross-checks (L2):**
  - Little's law, (the time turns spent in progress inside the window) / (the turns that ended in it), computed from
    when the last warm-up session ended (`t_full`). It should agree with the KM mean in lightly cut cells; in heavy
    cells it breaks down, which is reported as such.
  - The naive mean over finished turns.
  - Median for turns started in the first half of the window against the second half, a stationarity check.
- **By family:** EXPLAIN and MODIFY separately, plus a family-standardized curve.
  - This matters because the mix is skewed: slow cells reach few MODIFY turns, which are 1.1-3.4× longer.
